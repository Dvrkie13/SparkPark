SparkPark v0.3.0-alpha: components and their licenses
=====================================================

Every component in this folder, where it is, and its license. The full
license texts are in the licenses folder (and, where a component ships its
own, beside it too).

SparkPark itself
  SparkPark.exe, ui\ (except the font), recompiler\tools\sparkpark\,
  runtime\ (the SparkPark additions)
  License: CC0 1.0 (licenses\SparkPark-CC0.txt)
  https://github.com/Dvrkie13/SparkPark

PokePark Wiicompiled, by MildRain479

  (recompiler\tools\recomp\), the runtime compiled into

  https://github.com/MildRain479/PokePark-Wiicompiled
